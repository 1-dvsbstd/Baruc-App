Add a monthly pot to your FPL mini-league.

Baruc tracks the side competition FPL does not: Manager of the Month pots, an overall prize and who has won what. Set it up in minutes.

1

Find your league

Enter the numeric ID from your FPL classic mini-league.

Not sure how?
  1. Open FPL and go to Leagues & Cups.
  2. Open your classic mini-league standings in a browser.
  3. Copy the number after /leagues/ in the address. For example, /leagues/35999/standings means your ID is 35999.

Use the league ID, rather than the invitation code. If the FPL app hides the address, open FPL in your browser.

2

What’s the entry fee?

£5 minimum, in £5 increments. Baruc shows the full pool instantly.

Choose how to split the pot

Manager of the Month is what Baruc is built for. Overall-only stays available if you want it simple.

FPL already handles the overall table well. Baruc’s recurring option keeps the extra pots, period winners and winnings history in one place. Periods run in four-gameweek blocks, followed by the GW37–38 Final Sprint.

One last look

You can go back and change anything before creating. Creation locks the entry fee, prize plan and starting gameweek. Your shared link is view-only.
